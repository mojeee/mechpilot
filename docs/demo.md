# Demo script: design by writing, point, change, print

A ten-minute walkthrough of MechPilot with the assistant, for a live demo. Every step uses what
ships with the app; the assistant needs an API key (View > Assistant, or the Assistant tab). The
default model is Claude Opus 5.5. Nothing the assistant proposes is applied until you click
Accept, and every accepted proposal is one undo step (`U`).

## 0. Before the demo

- Start the app (`npm run dev` or the installed app), dark theme, white canvas if the projector
  is bright (View menu).
- Assistant tab: provider Claude, model Claude Opus 5.5, key stored (green "Key stored").
- Close any open file: File > New.

## 1. A part from one sentence (2D)

Type in the Assistant tab:

> A mounting plate 160 × 100 mm, R12 corners, four clearance holes for M8 on a 130 × 70 pattern,
> and a Ø40 H7 bore in the centre.

What to show: the proposal card lists what it will add, the claims say where each number comes
from (Ø9 for M8 from `library/holes/iso-273` medium series; H7 from ISO 286), and the drawing
shows the ghost preview. Click **Accept**. Press `U` once to show it is one undo step, then `REDO`.

## 2. Step by step to a 3D part

> Make it a 3D part: extrude the outline 12 mm, cut the holes through.

Accept. The 3D tab opens with the body; the Features tab shows the tree. Then:

> Add a 25 mm tall boss Ø60 around the bore on top, and a 2 mm fillet on the boss edges.

Accept, rotate the view. Then an impossible request, to show honesty:

> Fillet every edge with 20 mm.

The proposal is built in the background and fails in plain words ("the fillet of 20 mm does not
fit the 12 mm plate…"); nothing is applied.

## 3. Point at something and ask for a change

- In the 3D viewer, Alt+click the top face of the boss. A chip "Face … normal +Z" appears in the
  chat input.
- Type:

> Chamfer this face's outer edge 1 × 45°, and make the boss 30 mm tall instead.

Accept. Then in the 2D Model tab select the four hole circles (window selection), click
**Use selection**, and type:

> Change these to M10 clearance holes and move the pattern to 136 × 76.

Accept; the holes and the hole feature follow.

## 4. The drawing and the PDF

> Dimension the plate to ISO 129-1 (outline, hole pattern, bore with its H7 tolerance), put it on
> an A3 sheet at 1:1 with the title block filled in, and export the PDF.

Accept. The sheet tab opens, the PDF save dialog appears; save and open the PDF.

## 5. If something goes wrong

- No answer, or a key error: the Assistant tab says why in plain words; check the key and the
  model name.
- A proposal you do not like: **Reject**, or accept and press `U`.
- The assistant never edits silently: look for the card, nothing changes without Accept.
