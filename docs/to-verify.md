# To verify

Table values and rules from standards that could not be verified against the standard's text while
building. They are **not** in code (product rule 2). Each entry says where it would go once a copy
of the standard confirms it.

| Standard | Value or rule | Where it would go | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AutoCAD Color Index (DXF group 62) | The RGB values of the greys 8, 9 and 250–255 and the formula used for colours 10–249 in `shared/dxf/codes.ts` come from memory of AutoCAD's colour dialog, not from a published table. The nine basic colours 1–9 are the well-known ones. | `shared/dxf/codes.ts`, `aciToRgb` / `nearestAci` | compare with a published ACI table |
| DXF compatibility | The AC1015 (2000) and AC1009 (R12) output has only been checked by MechPilot's own reader and the unit tests. | `shared/dxf/write.ts` | open an exported file in AutoCAD, LibreCAD and DraftSight (owner, by hand) |
| ISO 128-2:2020 Annex D | The line type application numbers for mechanical engineering drawings (e.g. which "01.2.x" number covers visible edges, which "04.1.x" covers centre lines). The `use` texts in `library/line-types/iso-128-2.json` come from the former ISO 128-24 and are cited as "Annex D" without numbers. | `library/line-types/iso-128-2.json`, field `source` of each type | application numbers to confirm |
| ISO 5457:1999/Amd 1:2010 | A4 used in landscape (horizontal) position. Only DIN Media's summary of DIN EN ISO 5457 says Amd 1 allows it (Austrian Standards lists Table 1, Figure 1 and 4.1 as changed); the amendment's text was not seen. A4 is stored portrait only. | `library/sheets/iso-5457.json`, `sizes` (an A4 landscape entry) | confirm against the amendment's text |
| ISO 5457:1999, 4.2 and Figure 4 | Width of the grid reference border (the strip that carries the zone letters and numerals). Figure 4 seems to show 5 mm; the text gives no number. | `library/sheets/iso-5457.json`, `zoneField` | read Figure 4 in a clean copy |
| UNI EN ISO 7200:2004 | Italian names of the title-block fields. `nameIt` is MechPilot's own translation; the Italian edition was not available. | `library/title-blocks/iso-7200.json`, `fields[].nameIt` | compare with UNI EN ISO 7200 |
| ISO 3098-1:2015 | Nominal heights 1.8 to 20 mm and the type A/B multiples of h (Tables 1 and 2) were read from ISO 3098-0:1997, which the 2015 edition replaces with the same clause structure. | `library/lettering/iso-3098.json` | confirm the 2015 clauses 5.3 and 5.5 are unchanged |
| ISO 129-1:2018, 5.4.1, 5.5 and Annex A | Terminator forms and sizes (arrowhead 30° closed filled / closed / open with length h, 90° open, oblique stroke length h at 45°, point 0.5h) and extension lines extending about 8 line widths past the dimension line, optional gap of about 8 line widths. Taken from the ISO 129-1:2004 text (5.3, 5.4, Figure A.1), seen in an unofficial copy; the 2018 clauses were not in the preview. | `library/dimensioning/iso-129-1.json`, `arrowhead`, `extensionLineOvershoot`, `extensionLineGap` | confirm against ISO 129-1:2018 |
| ISO 129-1:2018 | Spacing between parallel dimension lines, distance of the first dimension line from the outline, gap between the dimension text and the dimension line, size of the origin circle. No number was found in the text seen; left out. MechPilot's defaults for these are its own choice and must be marked so in code. | `library/dimensioning/iso-129-1.json` (`minDimensionLineSpacing`, `firstDimensionLineDistance`, `textGap`) | find the values, or record them as MechPilot's choice |
| ISO 129-1:2018, Annex B (informative) | Chamfer notation (e.g. "2 × 45°" or "C2"). Not seen; left out. | `library/dimensioning/iso-129-1.json`, a `chamfer` entry | read Annex B |
| ISO 129-1:2018/Amd 1:2020 | Content of the amendment not seen. | `library/dimensioning/iso-129-1.json` | check what it changes |
| ISO 286-1:2010, Tables 2 and 3 | Fundamental deviations of shafts a to zc and holes F to ZC, K, M, N and Delta were read from ISO 286-1:1988 (IS 919 (Part 1):1993). Only holes A to E were cross-checked against the 2010 edition (ISO 286-2:2010 Tables 2 and 3). | `library/fits/iso-286.json`, `fundamentalDeviations` | confirm against the 2010 tables |
| ISO 286-1:2010 | Fundamental deviations above 500 mm, shaft j and hole J, and cd/ef/fg (CD/EF/FG) are not stored. Hole K above IT8 for sizes over 3 mm is blank in the 1988 table (stored as null). | `library/fits/iso-286.json`, `fundamentalDeviations` | add from the 2010 tables when needed |
| ISO 1101:2017, Annex F | Symbol and tolerance-frame proportions. The frame height 2h, line width h/10 and compartment rules come from ISO 7083:1983 Table 2 and 5.2; Annex F of ISO 1101:2017 was not seen. | `library/gdt/iso-1101.json`, `frame` | confirm against ISO 1101:2017 Annex F / current ISO 7083 |
| ISO 5459:2011 | Datum indicator: whether the triangle is filled or open in the current standard, and its proportions (ISO 7083:1983 Figures 16 and 17 not transcribed). The "filled or open" rule is from ISO 1101:1983, 8.1. | `library/gdt/iso-1101.json`, `datumIndicator` | read ISO 5459 |
| UNI EN ISO 1101:2017 | Italian names of the geometrical characteristics (`nameIt`, MechPilot's translation). | `library/gdt/iso-1101.json`, `characteristics[].nameIt` | compare with UNI EN ISO 1101 |
| ISO 21920-1:2021, Annex A | Proportions of the surface texture symbol (leg heights, line width, bar). Annex A was not in the preview; the app uses the ISO 1302:1978 Annex B.3 values (`legacyProportions`: d' = h/10, H1 = 5 and H2 = 10 for h = 3.5, ...). | `library/surface/iso-21920-1.json`, `symbol.proportions` | read Annex A |
| ISO 21920-1:2021, clause 9 | Orientation of the symbol and text on the drawing (read from the bottom or the right; pointing from outside the material) is taken from ISO 1302:1978, 5.1. | `library/surface/iso-21920-1.json`, `indication.orientation` | confirm against clause 9 |
| ISO 21920 / ISO 1302:2002 | Whether a current standard still gives a preferred Ra series. `raSeries` (0.025 to 50 µm) is ISO 1302:1978 Table 1 (roughness grades N1 to N12). | `library/surface/iso-21920-1.json`, `raSeries` | check ISO 1302:2002 / ISO 21920-1 |
