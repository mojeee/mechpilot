Open-source mechanical CAD for macOS, Windows and Linux

Production drawings to ISO standards, with 3D parts built in.

MechPilot is a desktop CAD app for mechanical design companies: draw, dimension and annotate to ISO 128, 129-1 and 7200, constrain your sketches, and export DXF and vector PDF. 3D parts with drawings linked to the model are being built next.

Free and open source. Runs on your computer. Your files stay yours.

Free and open source. Runs on your computer. Your files stay yours.

  • Local-first: no account, no cloud, no telemetry. The optional AI assistant will use your own API key.
  • An open, documented, versioned file format (plain JSON) that diffs well in Git. Autosave and crash recovery.
  • Millimetres in double precision; nothing is rounded on save, load or export.
  • Every drawing rule and table value cites its standard and clause. ISO by default, ASME as an option.
  • Apache-2.0. No GPL dependencies, checked in CI.

Files that open everywhere

DXF for drawings, STEP for parts, vector PDF at true scale for the shop floor, and a native format anyone can read.
