Open-source mechanical CAD, with AI inside

Draw to standard. Build in 3D. Ask in plain words.

MechPilot is free desktop CAD for production drawings to ISO standards, parametric sketches and 3D parts. Describe a part in one sentence, or point at a face and ask for a change: the assistant answers with a preview you accept or reject.

Free and open source. Runs on your computer. Your files stay yours.

01 · The assistant

AI inside. You decide.

The assistant reads your drawing and your parts and answers with a proposal: a highlighted preview, the list of what it changes, and where every number comes from. You stay the engineer who signs the drawing.

  1. Describe it

    Write the part in one sentence: “a 160 × 100 plate, R12 corners, four M8 clearance holes, a Ø40 H7 bore”. It comes back as a preview, and step by step it becomes a 3D part.

  2. Point at it

    Alt+click a face in the 3D view, or select lines on the drawing, and ask: “chamfer this edge 1 × 45°”, “make these M10 clearance holes”.

  3. Accept or reject

    Nothing changes until you click Accept. Every accepted proposal is one undo step. A request that cannot be built is answered in plain words, and nothing is applied.

  4. A source for every claim

    Each number says where it comes from: measured on your model, a standard and its clause, a library entry, or plainly “suggestion”.

  5. Your key, your provider

    Bring your own Claude, OpenAI or Gemini API key. There is no MechPilot server in between, and the app stays fully usable without AI.

  6. Only what you send

    Nothing leaves your computer except what the assistant sends to the model you chose. Your drawings stay on your disks.

03 · For engineering companies

Why MechPilot

Your drawings outlive any software licence. MechPilot is made for the drawing office that exchanges DXF and STEP with customers every day, signs drawings to ISO and must still open them in ten years: an open, documented file format on your own disks, standards with their sources, no fee per seat, and support from the people who build it when you need it.

  • €0per seat, for any use: Apache-2.0
  • 100,000entities panned and zoomed at 60 fps, checked by a performance test
  • 0accounts, servers or telemetry between you and your drawings

04 · Your files

Your files stay yours.

DXF for drawings, STEP for parts, vector PDF at true scale for the shop floor, and a native format anyone can read.

  • Local-first

    No account, no cloud, no telemetry. The optional AI assistant uses your own API key.

  • Open format

    An open, documented, versioned file format (plain JSON) that diffs well in Git. Autosave and crash recovery.

  • Exact

    Millimetres in double precision; nothing is rounded on save, load or export.

  • Sourced

    Every drawing rule and table value cites its standard and clause. ISO by default, ASME as an option.

  • Open source

    Apache-2.0. No GPL dependencies, checked in CI.
