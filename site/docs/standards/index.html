Standards used by MechPilot

MechPilot draws to ISO standards. This page lists each one, what the app takes from it, where that data lives and how well it has been checked. Every value in library/ names its standard and clause; values that could not be checked are left out and listed in to-verify.md (product rule 2).

How values were checked. "Standard text" means the value was read in the standard itself: the free previews published by iTeh (standards.iteh.ai), or the Indian Standards that adopt an ISO standard word for word and are published by Public.Resource.Org (law.resource.org). Some of those adoptions are of an older edition; where that is the case it is said below. "Secondary" would mean two independent handbooks or catalogues agree; only some material densities rely on that (see "Holes and materials" below).
