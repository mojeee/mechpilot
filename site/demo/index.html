Demo

Design by writing. Point. Change. Print.

A ten-minute walkthrough of MechPilot with the assistant: a part from one sentence, step by step to a 3D part, a change on a face you point at, and the dimensioned A3 drawing as a PDF. Nothing the assistant proposes is applied until you click Accept, and every accepted proposal is one undo step.

Chapters

  1. 01 A part from one sentence (2D)
  2. 02 Step by step to a 3D part
  3. 03 Point at something and ask for a change
  4. 04 The drawing and the PDF
  5. 05 If something goes wrong

Before the demo

  • Start the app (npm run dev or the installed app), dark theme, white canvas if the projector is bright (View menu).
  • Assistant tab: provider Claude, model Claude Opus 5.5, key stored (green "Key stored").
  • Close any open file: File > New.

01

Step 1 A part from one sentence (2D)

Type in the Assistant tab:

You typeA mounting plate 160 × 100 mm, R12 corners, four clearance holes for M8 on a 130 × 70 pattern, and a Ø40 H7 bore in the centre.

What to show: the proposal card lists what it will add, the claims say where each number comes from (Ø9 for M8 from library/holes/iso-273 medium series; H7 from ISO 286), and the drawing shows the ghost preview. Click Accept. Press U once to show it is one undo step, then REDO.

02

Step 2 Step by step to a 3D part

You typeMake it a 3D part: extrude the outline 12 mm, cut the holes through.

Accept. The 3D tab opens with the body; the Features tab shows the tree. Then:

You typeAdd a 25 mm tall boss Ø60 around the bore on top, and a 2 mm fillet on the boss edges.

Accept, rotate the view. Then an impossible request, to show honesty:

You typeFillet every edge with 20 mm.

The proposal is built in the background and fails in plain words ("the fillet of 20 mm does not fit the 12 mm plate…"); nothing is applied.

03

Step 3 Point at something and ask for a change

  • In the 3D viewer, Alt+click the top face of the boss. A chip "Face … normal +Z" appears in the chat input.
  • Type:

You typeChamfer this face's outer edge 1 × 45°, and make the boss 30 mm tall instead.

Accept. Then in the 2D Model tab select the four hole circles (window selection), click Use selection, and type:

You typeChange these to M10 clearance holes and move the pattern to 136 × 76.

Accept; the holes and the hole feature follow.

04

Step 4 The drawing and the PDF

You typeDimension the plate to ISO 129-1 (outline, hole pattern, bore with its H7 tolerance), put it on an A3 sheet at 1:1 with the title block filled in, and export the PDF.

Accept. The sheet tab opens, the PDF save dialog appears; save and open the PDF.

05

Step 5 If something goes wrong

  • No answer, or a key error: the Assistant tab says why in plain words; check the key and the model name.
  • A proposal you do not like: Reject, or accept and press U.
  • The assistant never edits silently: look for the card, nothing changes without Accept.

Try it yourself

Get MechPilot, open the Assistant tab, add your own Claude, OpenAI or Gemini API key, and type the first prompt:

You typeA mounting plate 160 × 100 mm, R12 corners, four clearance holes for M8 on a 130 × 70 pattern, and a Ø40 H7 bore in the centre.
