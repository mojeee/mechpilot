ISO 128-2 line types and line widths for technical drawings

The line types of technical drawings, what each is used for in mechanical engineering, and the widths a line may have.

Line widths

0.13 mm 0.18 mm 0.25 mm 0.35 mm 0.5 mm 0.7 mm 1 mm 1.4 mm 2 mm

Narrow lines are 0.5 × the width of wide lines. ISO 128-2:2020, 5.1 Line width: 'The width, d, of all types of lines shall be one of the following ... 0,13 mm; 0,18 mm; 0,25 mm; 0,35 mm; 0,5 mm; 0,7 mm; 1 mm; 1,4 mm; 2 mm.' and 'The widths of extra wide, wide and narrow lines are in the ratio 4:2:1.'

Patterns below are [element, gap, ...] in multiples of the line width d; dots use d.
