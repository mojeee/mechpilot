Note del file di dati

  • Densities vary with alloy, temper, casting and grade (plastics also with filler and processing). MechPilot computes mass from these values and shows it as approximate, with the material and the source of its density.
  • nameIt is MechPilot's own Italian translation; it was not taken from the Italian editions of the standards.
  • PTFE is not in this file: the datasheets read disagree (Ensinger TECAFLON PTFE natural 2.15 g/cm³, Plastock extruded virgin PTFE 2.16 g/cm³, BW Kunststoffe 2.15 to 2.25 g/cm³). See docs/to-verify.md.
